About Me

Developer. Designer.
Problem solver.

I'm Sachin Rathnayaka — an HNDIT IT undergraduate, Full Stack Web Developer and UI/UX designer from Tangalle, Sri Lanka. I build complete digital products, not just simple websites.

My work sits at the intersection of development, design and practical problem solving.

I started with the fundamentals — HTML, CSS, JavaScript — then moved into backend development with PHP and MySQL, and later into WordPress for businesses that need to move quickly. Alongside development, I design: creating interfaces in Figma and visual identities under my creative brand, BLACK STUDIO.

During a six-month internship at ACOM Information Technology, I worked inside a real software development workflow — building and refining web interfaces, debugging, working with databases and collaborating with a team. That experience shaped how I approach every project: understand the problem first, then design and build something that actually works.

I care about clean code, thoughtful interfaces and details most people never notice — because those are the details that make a product feel finished.
